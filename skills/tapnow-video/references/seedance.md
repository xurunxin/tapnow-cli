# Seedance direction

Assign each input a creative purpose: appearance, composition, movement, or sound. Describe what happens and how the camera follows it. A first-frame task should develop naturally from the supplied image; a reference task should state which qualities to borrow. Keep actions achievable within the configured duration. Inspect visual and audio alignment together.

Original example:

> Image 1 defines the red toy boat and its painted details. Over five seconds, the boat drifts toward the foreground as small ripples reach the wooden dock. The camera gently tracks backward to keep the boat in view. Quiet water and wind; no spoken words.

For first/last frames, describe the intermediate action needed to reach the ending. For edits, identify the intended change and retained scene. Reference ordering comes from the actual images/videos/audios arrays; `workflow prepare` shows the resulting role-tagged payload.

Reviewed 2026-09-10: [ByteDance Seedance 2.0 project](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance2_0) describes multimodal reference and audiovisual generation. It supports this directing approach, not every TapNow variant's parameter limits. Query TapNow for 2.0, Fast, Mini, and 2.5 separately.
