# H3 direction

Organize a shot around an observable opening, action, camera movement and ending. For demanding audiovisual prompts, separate the visual/action timeline, environmental sound, and background music. With first/last frames, explain a continuous transition and place the ending at the configured duration. Treat speech as exact content tied to a consistent speaker.

Original example:

> Visual/action: The paper crane in image 1 rests beside a window. It lifts its wings once, then settles. The camera slowly moves closer throughout the five-second shot, keeping the folds visible.
> Environmental sound: A soft paper rustle and distant rain.
> Music: None.

Use reference inputs to anchor appearance, movement or sound and explain that purpose. H3's reference mode and first-frame mode have different semantics; choose intentionally. Avoid importing another service's model flags or local inference tuning into TapNow params.
